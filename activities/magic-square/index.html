The Staircase / Outside-Square Method:
Step 1: Add 4 outer auxiliary squares on the 4 sides of the 3×3 grid.
Step 2: Place numbers 1-9 along 3 continuous diagonals.
Step 3: Transfer outside numbers 3 units straight across to their opposite inner squares!
Step 1: Notice how numbers 1, 2, 3 form the first diagonal; 4, 5, 6 form the center diagonal; and 7, 8, 9 form the third diagonal. Four numbers (1, 3, 7, 9) sit outside the 3×3 core.

1. Why Magic Constant S = 15?

• Sum of all numbers from 1 to 9:
1 + 2 + 3 + ... + 9 = 45

• In a 3×3 square, all numbers are divided equally across 3 rows.
• Therefore, each row must sum to:
45 ÷ 3 = 15

2. Why Center Cell MUST be 5?

• 4 lines pass through the center cell C: Row 2, Col 2, and 2 diagonals.
• Sum of these 4 lines = 4 × 15 = 60.
• This sum contains all 9 cells once, plus the center cell 3 extra times:
45 + 3C = 60 ➔ 3C = 15 ➔ C = 5

🌟 Even & Odd Number Rule:

• All 4 even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8) must occupy the 4 corners.
• All 4 odd numbers (1, 3, 7, 9) must occupy the 4 edge centers.
• The center is always the median odd number 5.
